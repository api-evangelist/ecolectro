A common refrain in the world of climate solutions is that there are no silver bullets, only silver buckshot. But if any climate technology comes close to a whole silver bullet, it’s the green hydrogen electrolyzer. Hydrogen is fuel that emits virtually zero greenhouse gas emissions when it is burned.
